Questions

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Tirzepatide is a prescription medicine. We ask about your prescription before confirming any order, and the dose you use must be the one your doctor prescribed.

How much is a kit?

Prices depend on the vial strength. Send an order request and we'll reply with the exact price, payment options and delivery to your area.

What side effects are common?

Nausea, diarrhea, constipation, less appetite and heartburn are common, especially early on or after a dose increase. The companion app lists warning signs that need a doctor right away.

Is my information private?

Yes. The self-check runs only on this page. Your health journal in the companion app stays on your own phone and is never sent to BLISS.

How do I pay and receive my kit?

After you send your request, we message you to confirm details, payment and delivery or pick-up.
