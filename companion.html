Reference dosing information

For FDA-approved Zepbound, under a clinician's prescription; this schedule must not be assumed to apply to an unapproved or differently prepared product.

TimingDoseNotes
Weeks 1–42.5 mg weeklyStarting dose
After 4 weeks5 mg weeklyIncrease only as prescribed
Later2.5 mg incrementsAt least 4 weeks between increases
Maintenance5, 10, or 15 mg15 mg weekly maximum
  • Inject once per week on the same day, at any time of day.
  • Missed dose: take within 96 hours. If more than 96 hours have passed, skip it and resume the regular schedule. Never double a dose.
  • Change dose day only when at least 72 hours separate doses.
  • For preparation and measuring, follow your dispensing pharmacist's product-specific instructions and contact your clinician when uncertain.

Monthly reminders

  • Rotate injection locations among abdomen, thigh and upper arm as instructed.
  • Follow the specific dispensed product's storage and beyond-use instructions; do not assume all mixed vials have the same shelf life.
  • Use a new sterile syringe and proper cleaning each time.
  • Oral contraceptive users may need an additional barrier method for four weeks after initiation and each escalation.
  • Tirzepatide should not be used during pregnancy. Discuss plans for pregnancy with your clinician.

Safety first

Watch for

Get emergency care

Call 911 in the Philippines or your local emergency number for swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat, trouble breathing, collapse or other symptoms of a severe allergic reaction.

Stop using and urgently contact a clinician

  • Severe, persistent abdominal pain, possibly spreading to your back, with or without vomiting.
  • Right upper abdominal pain, fever, yellowing eyes or skin, or pale stools.
  • Severe vomiting or diarrhea, very low urine output, or signs of dehydration.
  • Shakiness, sweating, confusion or racing heart, particularly with insulin or sulfonylurea drugs.
  • Neck lump, persistent hoarseness or difficulty swallowing.
  • Changes in vision, particularly with diabetes.
  • Significant worsening mood or thoughts of self-harm; seek urgent local help for immediate danger.

Common early effects

Nausea, diarrhea, constipation, vomiting, reduced appetite, heartburn and injection-site irritation. These can recur after dose escalation.

  • Eat smaller meals and stop when full.
  • Reduce high-fat or very sugary foods if they worsen symptoms.
  • Hydrate regularly; ask your clinician if you have fluid restrictions.
  • Report persistent, severe or concerning symptoms.

Before you begin

Tell your clinician about personal or family medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2, pregnancy, pancreatitis, gallbladder or kidney disease, severe digestive problems, diabetic eye disease and all current medicines.

This app provides tracking and general educational information, not a diagnosis, prescription, or dosing authorization. Only use medication prescribed and dispensed through legitimate clinical channels.
